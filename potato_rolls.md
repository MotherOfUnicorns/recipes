# Potato rolls

Great for burgers. I just copied [this recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WehQz1Hi7ZM) here so it's easier to reference.
This makes 12 pieces.


## Ingredients

- 585g all-purpose flour
- 200g milk
- 134g cooked and riced potatoes
- 47g potato cooking liquid
- 58g butter
- 1 large egg, about 57g
- 48g sugar
- 14g salt
- 4g instant yeast


## Preparations

- Boil and peel the potatoes using your method of choice. I cook them in a pressure cooker with some water and peel after they are cooked.
- Reserve some water from the potatoes.
- Rice or mash the potatoes and set aside to cool.
- Mix all ingredients. Let it rest for 20 minutes so the dough can hydrate.
- Knead for 1 minute, shape into a smooth ball. Cover and let rest for 1 hour.
- Stretch and fold twice, and then shape into a smooth ball. Cover and let it rest for 2 hours.
- Divide into 12 pieces (90g each). Roll each piece into a smooth ball. Cover with a wet towel and let rise for 1-2 hours as a final proof, till they double in size.
- Preheat oven at 190 degrees Celcius. Bake for 15 minuts until the top is golden brown.
- You can store them in the freezer for a few months.
